About Argentina

Argentina, located in South America, is known for its diverse geography—ranging from the Andes mountains and Patagonia to vast pampas grasslands and vibrant cities like Buenos Aires. It's famous for tango, soccer, wine, and incredible natural wonders like Iguazú Falls.
